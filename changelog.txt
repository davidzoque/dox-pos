== Dox POS changelog ==

Every version, newest first. The readme on WordPress.org shows only the recent ones.

= 0.60.0 =
* Fix: in stores that keep orders as posts (without HPOS), a new sale could return an earlier order instead of being recorded, because the check for a repeated sale used a query WooCommerce ignores there.
* Stores in several languages (Polylang or WPML): the register still shows each product once in Sell, Inventory, search, counts and Excel files, writes the stock log once on the main product and creates new products in the default language. Creating and editing the translations from the register (the language tabs, "Also create in the other languages", "Missing in English" and Create now) is now part of Dox POS Pro. Without it, editing a product changes only the main product and the translations are left as they are.
* New: if the store uses Polylang, WPML, Price Based on Country, CURCY, WOOCS or Aelia Currency Switcher and Dox POS Pro 0.42.0 or newer is not active, a short notice on the Dox POS settings and in the register says what Pro adds. It can be dismissed, once per user, and it never shows in the WordPress dashboard or on the website.
* Technical: new hooks for add-ons in the product form: the dox_pos_product_form_fields filter (slots name_before, name_after, price_after, short_before, short_after, description_after), the dox_pos_product_saved action, the dox_pos_product_response, dox_pos_product_edit_data and dox_pos_catalog_item filters, the dox_pos_products_settings and dox_pos_products_sanitize filters, the dox_pos_settings_products and dox_pos_rest_routes actions, and the productoListo, productoPayload, productoCargado... events of window.DoxPOS (see README.md).
* Technical: new hooks for add-ons that sell in another currency (Dox POS Pro does it with Price Based on Country zones). The plugin itself still sells in the store currency with the WooCommerce price: dox_pos_currency() (filter dox_pos_currency) replaces the scattered get_woocommerce_currency() calls; dox_pos_money_format() (filter dox_pos_money_format) gives the symbol, separators and decimals that dox_pos_money() and window.DoxPOS cfg.money use; dox_pos_product_price() (filter dox_pos_product_price, with the context sell, list or edit) is how the catalog, the cart, the order and shipping read a price; the dox_pos_order_problem filter lets an add-on refuse a sale, the dox_pos_order_before_totals action runs before the totals are calculated; reports skip other currencies when dox_pos_report_currency() says so; store credit keeps an optional currency per movement (the filter dox_pos_credit_currency); the dox_pos_shipping_rates, dox_pos_order_profit_pre, dox_pos_xlsx_money_format and dox_pos_history_response filters, the dox_pos_product_validate filter (an add-on can reject a product save with a WP_Error) and the historialConsulta and historialPintado events. Without an add-on every one of them returns exactly what it did before. See README.md.
* Technical: includes/multilingual.php keeps dox_pos_lang_plugin(), dox_pos_lang_default(), dox_pos_lang_where(), dox_pos_lang_main_id(), dox_pos_lang_twin_variation(), dox_pos_lang_term_args(), dox_pos_lang_assign_term() and dox_pos_lang_assign(); the rest moved to the Pro.

= 0.59.1 =
* Fix: with Polylang, saving a product from the register no longer replaces the photos of its translations when the photos of the main product did not change. A translation can have its own photos (uploaded apart, with the alt text in its language); they are kept, and the translation only takes the main product's photos when they change in the register.
* Technical: dox_pos_lang_copy_images() copies the photo and the gallery to a translation, each on its own, only when the main product's one differs from the last one it saw (_dox_pos_lang_imgs); the first time, a translation that already has a photo or a gallery keeps it.

= 0.59.0 =
* New: stores in several languages with Polylang or WPML. Each product exists once per language, and the register showed it twice (or three times): in Sell, Inventory, Edit product, the Excel file, the stock counts and the codes the product form suggests. Now only the products in the store's default language show, plus the ones with no language; a variation follows its product. Searching by the name in another language still finds the product, scanning the code of a translation sells the main product, and a web order placed in another language counts toward its main product in the best sellers.
* New: in Movements, when the translations share their stock, a change is written once, on the main product (also for a variation, matched by its attributes). The tools that keep the stock of the translations the same save the other translation too, and that second save was written as a second movement. When each translation keeps its own stock, its movements stay on it.
* New: a product created from the register is saved in the default language, whatever language filter the dashboard has.
* New: with Polylang, a product created in the register is also created in every other language of the store, linked as its translation and published, so it sells in every language from day one: same price, cost, photos, units, sizes and colors, and the categories in their translation (one with no translation is left out, and the register says so). The SKU of each translation ends in its language (VE-0012-EN), because WooCommerce does not allow the same SKU twice; the dox_pos_translation_sku filter changes it. It can be turned off in Settings > Products > Languages ("Also create in the other languages").
* New: the product form (creating and "Edit this product") has a tab per language (ES, EN...) above the name and the descriptions. Each tab keeps the text of its language and saves it to its own translation; a dot marks a language that still has the main language's text. Everything else is shared: editing the price, cost, photos, categories, units, sizes or colors from the register writes them to every translation (the dox_pos_lang_sync_price filter can keep the price apart).
* New: a translation that was left half made (the connection dropped or the server stopped while it was being created) is finished the next time, never made twice: when the register sends the same product again, when the product is saved from the register, or when it is opened in Edit product. A product missing a translation shows "The English version is missing" in the form with a Create now button (with the setting on, it is created as soon as the product opens), and Inventory and Edit product mark it "Missing in English".
* New: the product form has a Short description, the line WooCommerce shows under the price.
* New: the categories in the product form are only the ones in the main language, and a category created from the register is saved in the main language.
* WPML: the register shows each product once and counts it once, as above. Creating the translations, the language tabs and the Missing marker are Polylang only: with WPML the translations are still made in WPML.
* Stock still changes only through WooCommerce, so whatever the store uses to copy stock between translations (Polylang for WooCommerce, WooCommerce Multilingual or a snippet) keeps working. Polylang (free) does not copy it.
* Technical: new includes/multilingual.php with dox_pos_lang_plugin(), dox_pos_lang_where() (a NOT EXISTS on the language terms of Polylang or on icl_translations of WPML, for products and for variations through their parent), dox_pos_lang_main_id() (with Polylang, a variation of a translation maps to the variation of the main product: the one it was copied from, marked with _dox_pos_lang_of, or the one with the same attributes), dox_pos_lang_assign() and, for the translations, dox_pos_lang_sync() (WC_Admin_Duplicate_Product to create them, then the shared fields copied without writing to the kardex). Each copy is marked in the same save that creates it (_dox_pos_lang_of, _dox_pos_lang and _dox_pos_lang_pending until it is finished), so dox_pos_lang_sync() can adopt an unlinked copy (dox_pos_lang_orphan()) or finish a pending one instead of making another; dox_pos_lang_missing() feeds lang_missing in the edit data and nolang in the catalog and the edit list; POST /products/{id}/translations creates what is missing. Creating or finishing a translation takes a lock per product and language (dox_pos_lang_lock()), so two requests at once never make two; a translation in the trash is left alone. WPML only filters the lists. Without Polylang or WPML they make no queries and leave every query as it was.

= 0.58.0 =
* New: write off stock. In Inventory, add what is damaged, lost or given away and tap Write these off instead: it asks why (damaged or defective, lost or stolen, gift or sample, other), takes the units out of the inventory and notes the loss at what each unit cost. It is for products that never went through a sale, so there is no order to note the loss in. Only administrators and shop managers see the button.
* New: the written-off units show in Reports > Movements (filter Written off, with the reason and the cost per unit) and as a Written off figure in Reports > Daily cash, with the units and the loss.
* Technical: new reason writeoff in the stock log (dox_pos_stock_context( 'writeoff' )) whose note carries the reason; the loss is read from those rows (-delta times unit_cost), no new table. includes/writeoffs.php: dox_pos_write_off() (needs dox_pos_can_see_losses(), refuses more units than the stock and products that do not track stock, subtracts from the real size when the line carries one), dox_pos_writeoff_reasons() (cfg.writeoff, empty for who cannot), dox_pos_writeoffs_by_day() (totals writeoff, writeoff_units and writeoff_unknown in dox_pos_history_cash()) and REST route POST /writeoffs. New kind bajas in dox_pos_stock_kinds(). Needs a connection: it is not queued like the entries.

= 0.57.0 =
* New: Varies by. Each category says what its products vary by: up to two of the store's attributes, whatever they are. Clothes by Size and Color, thermoses by Capacity and Color, phone cases by Model, rings by Size and Material, shoes by Shoe size. The product form in the register keeps its grid: the first option goes in the rows and the second in the columns, and the titles change on their own (Capacity instead of Sizes). An option that is a color keeps its swatches; any other shows text buttons, like sizes. Set it in Settings > Products > Varies by, or from the register: when a product is created, the chosen category shows "Thermoses varies by Capacity and Color · Change it". From there a new attribute can be created with its values (the register warns when it looks like one the store already has, like Sizes and Size), and Add to Capacity… creates a value that is missing (a 750 ml), already marked.
* New: a store can have several size attributes, for example letters for clothes (Talla) and numbers for shoes (Talla calzado). In Settings > Products > Sizes and colors, mark every attribute that holds sizes. Until you change it they are detected by name, also the ones whose name starts or ends with size (Talla calzado, Shoe size); turning them all off means the store does not use sizes.
* New: a product created without colors (or without its second option) can get them from the register. When the product is edited, its category's option shows up: the units it already has go to the first value chosen, and the rest are added as new variations. Its SKUs and history stay as they were.
* Change: creating and editing use the product's own attributes, and the search, the sale, the inventory, the labels and the SKUs read any attribute ("500 ml · Red"). Shared units between sizes and pieces by actual size stay for sizes. A product that varies by three things or more still sells from the register and is edited in WooCommerce.
* Change: nothing to set up when updating. Each category keeps what it used: its size attribute (the one its products use, or the one set by hand) as the first option and the color as the second. A store with only Size and Color works exactly as before.
* Change: color swatches work with any theme, not only XStore. The register reads the shade of each color from XStore or from the most used swatch plugins (Variation Swatches for WooCommerce by GetWooPlugins or by CartFlows, ThemeHigh and VillaTheme), and a color created from the register is saved where the store's theme or plugin looks for it, so the shop shows its swatch too (a color attribute created from the register is created as a color type when the swatch plugin offers it). The shade set in the swatch plugin wins over an older one, colors with a photo swatch keep their photo and are not asked for a shade, and VillaTheme colors (saved as rgba) are read too. Short size labels are also read from ThemeHigh.
* Change: a color always goes in the columns of the product form, even when it is chosen as the first option in Varies by, because photos and shared units go by color. Choosing above the option that was below swaps them instead of emptying the second one.
* Change: when colors (or the first option) are added to a product that already exists, the first value chosen can be tapped again to remove it until saving, and saving says which value its units go to and asks to confirm. A product that had Color only as information (for the store's filter) can also get colors.
* Change: on a phone, Settings > Products > Varies by shows each menu across the screen (with Automatic, only one); the bottom bar of the product form hides while typing so the keyboard does not cover the field; the links of the form are easier to tap; Sell shows the whole SKU of each variation; the Counter lists sizes in order; and a value created with one product shows up in the next one without reloading.
* Fix: a subcategory whose products have no sizes (thermoses that vary by Capacity) now takes its parent's Varies by when the store has several size attributes.
* Fix: the warning about a similar attribute no longer mixes up short different words (Olor and Color, Tela and Talla, Marca and Manga), and using the existing one says which values were added.
* Fix: with the color on Automatic, the attribute called exactly Color keeps being the color even if another one with color in its name (Color de hilo) has more products.
* Fix: a color that already existed keeps its swatch or its image when it is chosen again from the register.
* Fix: with the size attribute on Automatic, a store with an empty "Talla" and its shoes in "Talla calzado" got the empty one, and the product form offered sizes nobody uses. Among several, the one that the most products have now comes first.
* Change: a color without a shade (created with New attribute…, or in a store without swatches) can get one from the register. When a color attribute is created with its values, each value shows a box to pick its shade; and when a color without a shade is chosen in a product, the form offers to pick it (or Not now). A color that already has a shade or an image keeps it.
* Change: changing Varies by from the register says clearly that it affects the next products of the whole category (and of its subcategories without their own), with how many products it has today, which stay as they are. If none of them varies, it offers Make it not vary; after saving it says how many subcategories changed too.
* Change: faster with several size attributes. What the products of each category use is counted once and kept until a product, a variation or a category changes, instead of on every request; and Settings > Products > Varies by works it out once for all the categories instead of once per category.
* Fix: after saving changes to a product, only the Saved window shows. The Create another window of a new product came out on top of it.
* Fix: saving Settings no longer undoes a Varies by changed from the register while the settings page was open: only the categories changed on the page are saved.
* Fix: closing the "looks like an existing attribute" warning no longer creates the attribute; it has Use it, Create anyway and Cancel. Saving Varies by from the register keeps the values already marked if the rows did not change.
* Fix: when colors are added to an existing product, marking a value, typing its units, removing it and marking it again no longer overwrites the units the product already had. Retrying a save that failed halfway does not create the same variations twice.
* Technical: new option key cat_vary in dox_pos_products (category => up to two taxonomies, or array( '-' )). dox_pos_category_vary_map() resolves it walking up the parents: the first category with cat_vary wins; one with cat_size or detected sizes (0.56) gives [its size, color]; nothing gives [size_attr, color_attr]. dox_pos_vary_axes() puts the first option in rows and the second in columns (a lone color goes to columns); dox_pos_product_axes() reads a product's own variation attributes. dox_pos_product_model() keeps its signature and keys (sizes, colors, size_attr) and adds color_attr; edit data adds add_size_attr and add_color_attr, and saving accepts adopt_size or adopt_color (dox_pos_adopt_axis()). products/form adds attrs (every global attribute with its values) and vary per category. New REST routes products/attributes (wc_create_attribute, error dox_pos_parecido with similar and label) and products/category-vary; products/sizes takes any attribute and an optional hex. Varies by fixes: dox_pos_vary_color_last() keeps a color attribute second (used by dox_pos_vary_axes(), dox_pos_set_category_vary() and the settings sanitize); dox_pos_category_vary_map() keeps walking up past a category whose products have no size (found '') and only stops at a detected size; dox_pos_adopt_axis() turns a non-variation attribute into a variation one (wp_set_object_terms without append) and runs after name, price and categories are validated; dox_pos_attribute_likeness() needs the same first letter and 5+ letters for distance 1, 8+ for distance 2; dox_pos_attribute_taxonomy() prefers an exact name; dox_pos_color_attributes( true ) resets after creating an attribute; products/attributes returns added, cuts the slug by bytes, skips reserved slugs, checks the new id and caps name and values; products/sizes writes the hex only on a new term, ThemeHigh only on a color-type attribute and VillaTheme only when its term has no type or a color type and no colors yet; the product form transient is deleted on woocommerce_attribute_added/updated/deleted and created/edited/delete_term of product_cat and pa_ taxonomies; dox_pos_format_product() sorts variations by size inside each color. Swatches: dox_pos_term_hex( $term_id, $tax ) reads st-color-swatch, product_attribute_color, cfvsw_color, product_{tax}, vi_wpvs_terms_params[color] and color, in that order (outside XStore only values with #); dox_pos_set_term_hex() writes to every active system from dox_pos_swatch_systems() (XStore theme, WOO_VARIATION_SWATCHES_PLUGIN_FILE, CFVSW_VER, THWVSF_VERSION, VI_WOO_PRODUCT_VARIATIONS_SWATCHES_VERSION; none: st-color-swatch); dox_pos_term_label() reads st-label-swatch, then ThemeHigh's product_{tax}. SKU codes are learned from any attribute (first option, then color). Pool names and real-size cells read the column attribute of the variation (dox_pos_variation_column()). Catalog variations add fila (the first option) and put other attributes between the size and the color in the label. Review: new REST route products/swatch (dox_pos_set_value_hex(): attr, id, hex; only on a color attribute and only when the term has no shade, else kept: true). dox_pos_category_usage() replaces the per-request query of dox_pos_category_size_found(): it keeps found (size per category, with 2+ size attributes) and varied (products with variations per category, summed up the parents) in the transient dox_pos_category_usage for a day, deleted by dox_pos_forget_category_usage() on woocommerce_new/update/delete/trash_product(_variation), set_object_terms of product_cat, trashed_post, untrashed_post, deleted_post and created/edited/delete_term of product_cat. dox_pos_category_size_map() and dox_pos_category_vary_map() take $own = false to skip each category's own setting (Settings builds the Automatic labels with one map). products/form categories add varied and parent. The register's crearProducto() shows modalProductoGuardado() or modalProductoCreado(), not both. = 0.52.0 =
* New: pieces by actual size. In a group of sizes that share units, the product form says how many pieces carry each size on their tag (Pieces by actual size), checks that they add up to the units of the group, and warns when a group is not split yet or does not add up. Sell, Inventory and the Counter say "4 in the group · 2 with tag 12-18 months" instead of looking like 4 of each size.
* New: when a size of a group is sold or put on layaway, the order line proposes which piece leaves (the closest actual size with pieces; if two are equally close, the one with more) and it can be changed right there. That piece goes down, the order's "Actual size" says the one that left, and cancelling, releasing or returning puts it back. Website orders take the closest piece when they are paid. A stock entry asks which tag the pieces came with. The stock history says which piece each movement was.

* A new product can carry one total for all its sizes, the way many shops keep their stock: under Units, "One total for all". The store then discounts from that total whichever size is sold, and you can spread it by size later from Edit one.

= 0.28.1 =
* An add-on can choose which tab the register opens on when there is no address to follow (Dox POS Pro uses it to open on Today for whoever manages the shop). Everyone else keeps opening on Sell.

= 0.28.0 =
* Tap a product anywhere (the assistant, the history, the stock entries) and its card opens right there: photo, code, price, cost and what each unit leaves for those who manage the shop, units per size (or the shared total), category, and the buttons to edit it or see it in the store. Before, it jumped straight to the editor.
* The loss on an order stands out: a proper "Note a loss" button on the detail, a red tag with the amount once noted, and the same tag on the orders list and in the history. The profit comes out in colour: green when it reaches the shop's target margin, amber when it falls short, red when it is low.

= 0.27.0 =
* Losses. From the order detail, an administrator or shop manager can note what an order cost beyond the goods (a shipment that cost more than what was charged, a freight refunded, a repair), with the reason. It comes off the profit of that sale, shows in the history, the cash report and their Excel files, and the order keeps a note of who wrote it.
* Your own payment methods. Settings > Sales has "Add payment method": a second Nequi account, Daviplata, Addi, whatever the shop takes. They can be turned off, renamed and removed, and a sale recorded with one counts as paid.
* Sizes that share one stock total. When a product keeps one total for all its sizes (the parent manages the stock and the sizes inherit it), the register says so ("5 left for all sizes") instead of repeating the number on every size and adding them up, the order cannot take more than that total across sizes, and the message when it does not fit names the product and what is really left. Recording stock on one of those sizes says it goes to the shared total, and the product editor can spread that total by size so each one carries its own units from then on.

= 0.21.0 =
* Costs and profit. Each product carries a cost per unit in the WooCommerce cost field: set it when you create or edit a product in the register, or all at once from the inventory Excel with the Cost column filled in. Every stock entry carries what each unit cost and recalculates the product's weighted average cost. Every sale freezes the cost in its order, so raising a cost later does not change history. The history shows the profit and the margin, the stock ledger shows the unit cost of each movement, and the Excel files carry the cost, profit and value at cost columns. Only administrators and shop managers see any of it.
