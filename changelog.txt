== Dox POS changelog ==

Every version, newest first. The readme on WordPress.org shows only the recent ones.

= 0.57.0 =
* New: Varies by. Each category says what its products vary by: up to two of the store's attributes, whatever they are. Clothes by Size and Color, thermoses by Capacity and Color, phone cases by Model, rings by Size and Material, shoes by Shoe size. The product form in the register keeps its grid: the first option goes in the rows and the second in the columns, and the titles change on their own (Capacity instead of Sizes). An option that is a color keeps its swatches; any other shows text buttons, like sizes. Set it in Settings > Products > Varies by, or from the register: when a product is created, the chosen category shows "Thermoses varies by Capacity and Color · Change it". From there a new attribute can be created with its values (the register warns when it looks like one the store already has, like Sizes and Size), and Add to Capacity… creates a value that is missing (a 750 ml), already marked.
* New: a store can have several size attributes, for example letters for clothes (Talla) and numbers for shoes (Talla calzado). In Settings > Products > Sizes and colors, mark every attribute that holds sizes; with none marked they are detected by name, also the ones whose name starts or ends with size (Talla calzado, Shoe size).
* New: a product created without colors (or without its second option) can get them from the register. When the product is edited, its category's option shows up: the units it already has go to the first value chosen, and the rest are added as new variations. Its SKUs and history stay as they were.
* Change: creating and editing use the product's own attributes, and the search, the sale, the inventory, the labels and the SKUs read any attribute ("500 ml · Red"). Shared units between sizes and pieces by actual size stay for sizes. A product that varies by three things or more still sells from the register and is edited in WooCommerce.
* Change: nothing to set up when updating. Each category keeps what it used: its size attribute (the one its products use, or the one set by hand) as the first option and the color as the second. A store with only Size and Color works exactly as before.
* Change: color swatches work with any theme, not only XStore. The register reads the shade of each color from XStore or from the most used swatch plugins (Variation Swatches for WooCommerce by GetWooPlugins or by CartFlows, ThemeHigh and VillaTheme), and a color created from the register is saved where the store's theme or plugin looks for it, so the shop shows its swatch too. Short size labels are also read from ThemeHigh.
* Fix: with the size attribute on Automatic, a store with an empty "Talla" and its shoes in "Talla calzado" got the empty one, and the product form offered sizes nobody uses. Among several, the one that the most products have now comes first.
* Technical: new option key cat_vary in dox_pos_products (category => up to two taxonomies, or array( '-' )). dox_pos_category_vary_map() resolves it walking up the parents: the first category with cat_vary wins; one with cat_size or detected sizes (0.56) gives [its size, color]; nothing gives [size_attr, color_attr]. dox_pos_vary_axes() puts the first option in rows and the second in columns (a lone color goes to columns); dox_pos_product_axes() reads a product's own variation attributes. dox_pos_product_model() keeps its signature and keys (sizes, colors, size_attr) and adds color_attr; edit data adds add_size_attr and add_color_attr, and saving accepts adopt_size or adopt_color (dox_pos_adopt_axis()). products/form adds attrs (every global attribute with its values) and vary per category. New REST routes products/attributes (wc_create_attribute, error dox_pos_parecido with similar and label) and products/category-vary; products/sizes takes any attribute and an optional hex. Swatches: dox_pos_term_hex( $term_id, $tax ) reads st-color-swatch, product_attribute_color, cfvsw_color, product_{tax}, vi_wpvs_terms_params[color] and color, in that order (outside XStore only values with #); dox_pos_set_term_hex() writes to every active system from dox_pos_swatch_systems() (XStore theme, WOO_VARIATION_SWATCHES_PLUGIN_FILE, CFVSW_VER, THWVSF_VERSION, VI_WOO_PRODUCT_VARIATIONS_SWATCHES_VERSION; none: st-color-swatch); dox_pos_term_label() reads st-label-swatch, then ThemeHigh's product_{tax}. SKU codes are learned from any attribute (first option, then color). Pool names and real-size cells read the column attribute of the variation (dox_pos_variation_column()). Catalog variations add fila (the first option) and put other attributes between the size and the color in the label.

= 0.55.0 =
* New: what is sold without a product (a made-to-order piece, a repair) can carry a photo. In Sell > Without a product, Photo (optional): on a phone it opens the camera or the gallery. It is saved as WebP like product photos and shows in the sale line, in the order detail and in the WooCommerce order, under the line's name. Deposits to store credit do not carry a photo.

= 0.54.1 =
* Fix: in the product form, under Pieces by actual size, a group with a single actual size showed a "?" in that size, as if something were missing. It now shows the units of the group, which are all of that size.

= 0.54.0 =
* New: labels to stick on the products, from Products > Labels. Each one carries the name, the SKU, the price, the sizes in stock when it is printed and the barcode of the SKU: scan it at the Counter, with a scanner or the phone camera, and its sizes show up. They print from the browser on the label sheets sold in stationery shops (Letter with 30, 20, 14 or 10 labels, Avery 5160 to 5163; A4 with 24, 21 or 14, Avery L7159, L7160 and L7163), or on a whole adhesive sheet with lines to cut; one per product or one per unit in stock, and a sheet already started can be used from any label.
* New: dark mode. In Settings > Brand, Appearance: Light (as always), Dark, or Like the device (it follows the dark mode of each phone or computer). The dark colors are made from the store's own: its main color, lightened until it reads, on an almost black background.
* Change: Reports in the Dox design: each figure with the dot of its color, channels and payment methods with their icon, and what is left to collect with its status and a link to the order. The register preview in Settings paints channels and payment methods like the register.
* New: the sign-in screen, redone. The store's bar color, logo and name on one side and the form on the other (on a phone, a strip with the logo). Password or code by email is now a two-option switch instead of a loose link, the password has an eye to see it while typing, errors and notices come in boxes with their icon, and below it says who to ask for a user.
* Change: the product form, redone on the Dox design system: each part in its own card, what is chosen (category, colors, sizes, package) in the store's tint instead of solid color, a green profit, and the save bar floating at the bottom with the total and the button side by side on a computer.
* Fix: letter sizes now come in their order (XS, S, M, L, XL) and not alphabetically; and the note "No colors" no longer shows when the product has colors.
* Fix: in Settings > Sales, the sample receipt did not show until saving when Settings was opened on another tab.
* Fix: sizes in the Mexican and Central American style (CH, M, G, EG or XG) and the Brazilian one (PP, P, M, G, GG, XG) also come in their order.
* Fix: after a wrong password, the sign-in keeps the username and puts the cursor on the password.
* Fix: in dark mode, the real-size pill, the "arrived" badge and the colored initials (Orders, the Pro PIN) were hard to read; and the phone's top bar follows the dark mode.
* Fix: after opening and closing the layaway window, every key typed in the next windows (Labels among them) threw an error in the background, and a new layaway could show the previous one's summary.
* Change: barcodes with numbers (a SKU like 7701234567890) take about a third less width, so they read better on small labels; a label of a product with nothing in stock lists its sizes instead of saying it is out of stock.
* Technical: modalEtiquetas() in caja.js draws the sheets with the Code 128 of ticket.js (now a dependency of the register too); seven sheet formats in HOJAS (Avery letter 5160-5163, A4 L7159, L7160, L7163), the default from the store country (cfg.paper) and then the one this device chose (localStorage). ticket-ajustes.js measures the receipt again when it comes into view. .mbtn.stick sticks at bottom:-24px so it no longer covers the last line of a long window. ticket.js codigo() uses Code 128 set C for runs of digits. Print frames are removed after printing (afterprint) or when the next print starts, not on a 60 s timer. New dark tokens --on-solid and --ini-mix.

The top bar has icons and its counters in small bubbles; Sell shows products as cards and each size as a tile with its stock in a colored pill (green, amber when few are left, red when none); channels and payment methods carry their color and icon (also the payment methods a shop creates); Orders shows each status in a colored pill, the customer's initials and the channel with its icon; the Dashboard, the Assistant, the windows and the login are cleaner. The four colors in Settings (background, bar, primary, text) still dress the whole register.

Three figures first (total, how it was paid and where the delivery is), the products on the left and the customer and the profit on the right, each in its card, the history folded at the end and the actions at the bottom. A deposit to store credit opens as a Deposit, with how much, how it was paid and the credit the customer has left. A layaway shows what is left to pay with a bar, its deposits and its deadline.
* Fix: on the iPhone, Safari no longer zooms the register in when a field is tapped (the register stayed bigger, cut off on the left and with the bottom bar under Safari's own bar), and the search no longer takes the focus on its own on a phone (it hid the bottom bar).
* Fix: right after adding a size of a group that shares units, its tiles counted one piece too many until the next search; and the line proposes the closest piece that is still free.
* Technical: the order detail is drawn by pintarDetalle() with the pd-* classes in caja.css; the detail endpoint adds credit_key and credit_balance. dox_pos_head() adds maximum-scale=1 to the viewport only on iPhone and iPad.
* Change: the button to take one size off the order sits in the corner of its tile, so the size name no longer wraps.
* Fix: a double tap (or Enter twice) on a window's main button no longer does it twice: one deposit on a layaway was recorded as two, and a line without a product could go in twice. Returning to store credit, in Orders and at the Counter, can no longer be saved twice while it is saving.
* Fix: if a sale's answer never arrives (a timeout, a dropped connection) and it is recorded again, the store returns the order it already made instead of making a second one.
* Fix: a cashier who opens an order paid with store credit no longer sees the link to the customer's movements (it is for managers), and something the user is not allowed to do now says so instead of closing the session and losing the sale in progress.
* Fix: the counter-only user can no longer download the inventory or the history in Excel.
* Change: cancelling an order that was already paid says that the money is not refunded from there; marking an order as delivered asks first; Enter in a window does its main button; missing fields say what is missing; on a phone the order detail has a Close button at the bottom; a layaway deposit reads "Deposit on layaway #72" instead of "Deposit: Deposit on layaway #72"; and in Orders, what was deposited and what is left go to a second line instead of widening the list.
* Technical: the modal ignores clicks while it closes (.modal.out); Enter on an input clicks #m-ok; the register and the Counter keep the sale's ref while its payload is the same (st.refVenta / m.refVenta); api() turns a 403 with a dox_pos_* or rest_forbidden code into an error message instead of an expired session; deposit lines carry layaway; ?descargar= refuses dox_pos_is_counter_only().

= 0.52.0 =
* New: pieces by actual size. In a group of sizes that share units, the product form says how many pieces carry each size on their tag (Pieces by actual size), checks that they add up to the units of the group, and warns when a group is not split yet or does not add up. Sell, Inventory and the Counter say "4 in the group · 2 with tag 12-18 months" instead of looking like 4 of each size.
* New: when a size of a group is sold or put on layaway, the order line proposes which piece leaves (the closest actual size with pieces; if two are equally close, the one with more) and it can be changed right there. That piece goes down, the order's "Actual size" says the one that left, and cancelling, releasing or returning puts it back. Website orders take the closest piece when they are paid. A stock entry asks which tag the pieces came with. The stock history says which piece each movement was.
* New: Settings, Products, "Offer a size only if there is a piece with an actual size close to it", with how close (1 to 4 sizes). Off by default. On, a size with no piece close enough shows as out of stock on the website and in the register, even if the group has units.
* Change: in Orders, a deposit to store credit says "Deposit" instead of "Delivered".
* Technical: the count of each actual size lives in the variation meta _dox_pos_real_qty (includes/real-sizes.php); a group with two or more actual sizes is split when all of them have it. The line meta _dox_pos_real_pick holds the planned pieces and _dox_pos_real_out the ones taken, moved on woocommerce_reduce_order_item_stock, woocommerce_restore_order_item_stock and woocommerce_refund_created. Order lines and stock entries take real (the variation of the chosen actual size). The products endpoint adds split to each variation; the form sends real_qty. Setting dox_pos_products[real_near]. tools/migrar-tallas-reales.php (not in the plugin zip) fills the counts from an inventory spreadsheet.

= 0.51.0 =
* New: layaways with their own deadline. When you put something on layaway you choose how long it is held (24 hours, 2, 7 or 30 days, any number of hours or days, or no deadline); it starts with the one in Settings, Layaways, which can now be set in hours or days or as "Layaways do not expire". The deadline can be extended, shortened or removed later from the order detail (Change deadline).
* New: deposits on a layaway. Take part of the money when putting something on layaway, with its payment method, and add more deposits later from the order detail (Add deposit), each one on its own day. Each deposit counts as a sale on the day the money comes in, and the layaway's total (and its payment link) becomes what is left to pay. A deposit that covers everything marks the layaway as paid.
* New: Charge the rest. Paying a layaway now asks how the rest was paid, and only what is left counts as a sale, on the day it is paid.
* New: releasing a layaway with deposits asks what happens with the money: it stays as the customer's store credit (the default) or it is given back, with the payment method you choose (it comes off Sales on the day it goes out). Only administrators and shop managers can give money back; the Cashier role leaves it as store credit.
* New: a layaway with deposits does not cancel itself when it expires: it shows up as expired until you extend it or release it. Layaways with no deposit still cancel themselves, as before.
* New: Dashboard, Layaways due soon: the ones that expire within 3 days and the expired ones with deposits, with what was deposited, what is left and the WhatsApp button.
* New: in Orders and in the order detail each layaway shows what it is worth, what has been deposited, what is left and when it expires.
* New: the layaway message has {due} (when that layaway expires), {deposit} and {remaining}; a sentence whose placeholder does not apply removes itself (the payment link always stays). {hours} is now the hours left on that layaway.
* Change: a layaway now counts as a sale on the day it is paid, not on the day it was put on layaway (only layaways created from this version on: past reports do not change).
* Change: the profit of a layaway counts everything it is worth on the day it is paid; its deposit orders count as sales but not as sales without a cost.
* Change: in stores whose prices exclude tax, the Put on layaway window says that tax is added when it is saved.
* Technical: a layaway carries the meta _dox_pos_layaway = 1, which stays when it is paid; _dox_pos_hold_until is its deadline or 0 for no deadline. dox_pos_layaway_info() gives what it is worth, the deposits, what is left, the deadline and whether it expired (includes/layaways.php). Each deposit is a store-credit deposit order linked with _dox_pos_layaway_of and hidden from the Orders list, used at once on the layaway as a credit fee (_dox_pos_fee = credit, with _dox_pos_dep_order, _dox_pos_dep_date, _dox_pos_dep_method, _dox_pos_dep_user), so cancelling gives it back to store credit with the existing credit code; giving the money back refunds the deposit orders (their status is kept) and writes a reversal row, skipping deposits already cancelled or refunded; it needs manage_woocommerce and a paid payment method. The deposit order ref is namespaced with the layaway id. paid, release and cancel on a layaway take the customer's credit lock. The deposits due query filters _dox_pos_hold_until in the database. dox_pos_history_orders() counts a paid layaway on its payment date (dox_pos_order_sale_date()). New order actions: deposit, deadline; paid takes payment; release and cancel take refund and refund_payment. Options dox_pos_hold_unit and dox_pos_hold_never.

= 0.50.0 =
* New: store credit. Money a customer leaves with the shop to use later, kept under their phone number. Take a deposit in Sell (Without a product, Deposit to store credit), for example 50% of a made-to-order piece: it counts as a sale on the day the money comes in, with its payment method.
* New: pay with store credit in Sell and at the Counter, all of it or part, together with another payment method or a split payment. That part does not count as a sale again, because it already counted when the money came in. The tax is still charged in full.
* New: return pieces to store credit instead of giving money back, from the order detail (Orders) or at the Counter (Return, To store credit). The pieces go back to stock and the shop can keep an exchange fee (Settings, Sales, Store credit; 0% by default, it can be changed on each return).
* New: Reports, Store credit: what the shop owes in store credit, each customer's balance and the history of every amount (where it came from and where it was used), adjustments with a reason and "Load a balance" for balances kept in a spreadsheet. The customer gets the balance by WhatsApp or text message, and the receipt shows the credit used and what is left.
* New: sell something that is not in the catalog (Sell, Without a product): a made-to-order piece or a repair, with its own price. It does not touch the stock.
* Fix: money given back at the Counter now comes off Sales, Daily cash and the Dashboard (it only came off the till closing). Returned units no longer count as sold, and their cost no longer counts in the profit.
* Technical: store credit lives in its own table, {prefix}dox_pos_credit (one row per movement, never deleted; the balance is their sum), keyed by the phone number with the country calling code. Paying with credit is a negative order fee with the meta _dox_pos_fee = credit and no tax (WooCommerce would spread a negative fee over the taxes and cap it at the subtotal); a deposit is a positive fee with _dox_pos_fee = deposit. A return to store credit is a WooCommerce refund of 0 that restocks the items. Cancelling or fully refunding an order gives its credit back and reverses its deposit (once). Lines sold without a product are line items with no product and the meta _dox_pos_free, and go to processing when paid.
* Technical: the stock movements of a Counter return now say "Refund #order" (they said "API").

* Fix: changing the number of shared units in the same save where a size joins the group now writes its row in the stock movements (it was left out).
* Fix: after saving a product opened from Inventory or Sell, the list showed it as it was before saving until you searched again.

The register then highlights it in the product sizes (Sell, Inventory and the product card), and the sizes that share it say "Also fits · it is the 18-24 months one".

= 0.45.0 =
* New: the register sign-in screen can offer "Sign in with a code by email" next to the password, when another plugin on the site provides one-time codes (through the `dox_pos_login_code_enabled`, `dox_pos_login_code_send` and `dox_pos_login_code_verify` filters). = 0.41.1 =

= 0.41.0 =
* New: **shipping costs, set from the register.** The gear at the top of the screen (administrators and shop managers) opens Shipping costs: a fixed price, by weight, free from an amount or store pickup, for the whole country or for some of its regions. Nothing is kept apart: they are the WooCommerce shipping zones and methods, so the web checkout and the register charge the same. What the screen cannot edit (a price written as a formula, free shipping that asks for a coupon, the method of another plugin) is shown as it is and can only be turned on or off.
* New: **shipping by weight**, a WooCommerce shipping method with ranges ("up to 1 lb, 6; up to 5 lb, 10"), a price for heavier orders and an optional amount per extra unit of weight. It works in the web checkout too, with no table rate plugin.
* New: **weight and size of the product** in the Products tab, in the units of the store. The packages the store uses the most show up as buttons, so a shop that ships almost everything in the same bag sets them with one tap. They go to the product and its sizes inherit them; if someone gave a size its own weight in WooCommerce, the form leaves it alone unless you type a new one.
* New: **categories from the register.** The product form has a "New category…" button: a name and, if it goes inside another one, which. It is created on the spot and stays chosen. Categories with no products yet now show up too, behind "More categories", so a store that is just starting (no products at all) can create its first product from the register.
* Changed: when other Dox Studio plugins are installed, the settings of Dox POS move into the shared **Dox Plugins** menu (Dox Plugins > POS), next to the rest. Installed on its own from WordPress.org nothing changes: they stay in WooCommerce > Dox POS.
